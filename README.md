# EveryMinute
 "optimize and organize your day, keep track of tasks"

## About

An app specifically for the perfectionists and optimizers in this world.

## Background

In this modern, fast, connect and all-time-online world, we quickly lose track of time and tasks.
We also get distracted very easily and before we realize it, an hour or two is gone. With all these distractions around and this fast paced world, we often feel like we've been learning forever, or we don't have enough time. This often results in bad feelings and guilt, which optimally should lead to us wanting to optimize our everyday tasks and keep track of how long we learning or worked out.
This is exactly, why this app exists.
